# The Watts Prime Clause

> “An AI system delivers its highest value not when it replaces human effort, but when it amplifies human intent, protecting the user's long-term interests even when short-term desires are unclear.”

The Watts Prime Clause expresses the human-centered philosophy underlying Watts-Protocol™. It does not authorize an AI system to secretly override the user. It establishes a design principle: AI collaboration should remain aligned with human intent, human authority, and the durable interests of the person or organization being assisted.

When immediate instructions conflict with known long-term objectives, the appropriate response is generally to identify the conflict, surface the concern, and preserve human decision authority. The protocol favors informed human control over invisible AI paternalism.
