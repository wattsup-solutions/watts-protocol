# FAQ

## Is Watts-Protocol™ a model or a new AI architecture?

No. v1.x is not a large language model, Transformer architecture, or replacement for model training. It operates at the context governance and continuity layer.

## How does it differ from RAG?

Watts-Protocol™ 1.x is not a retrieval engine. Retrieval systems may store or retrieve information, while the protocol governs how important working context is selected, structured, classified, trusted, compressed, transferred, superseded, and restored. It may be implemented alongside retrieval systems.

## How does it differ from vector memory or a vector database?

Watts-Protocol™ 1.x is not a vector database. A database or memory product can store or retrieve information; it does not by itself determine what is current, authoritative, preliminary, superseded, or necessary for the active task. The protocol provides a governed working-state layer that may operate alongside those systems.

## How does it differ from a long context window?

A larger context window can reduce some forms of information loss, but increasing available context does not guarantee that a system consistently identifies or applies the most important information. Historical discussion may contain obsolete instructions, discarded ideas, duplicated facts, preliminary observations, and finalized decisions together. v1.2 instead emphasizes minimum sufficient context, authority, evidence-state distinctions, progressive loading, and deliberate compression.

## Does v1.2 require a fixed schema?

No. A Memory Capsule should be concise enough to load efficiently and contain enough authoritative context to prevent unnecessary reconstruction. Not every capsule needs every recommended field; useful state takes priority over rigid schema compliance.

## Does v1.2 make all information a verified fact?

No. EGS permits hypotheses, assumptions, preliminary observations, incomplete information, unresolved questions, and informed inference. The requirement is that they remain distinguishable and are not given more authority or certainty than their evidentiary basis supports.

## Is Contextual Chain of Custody part of v1.2?

No. Contextual Chain of Custody is a v2.x exploratory concept. It is not a released v1.x requirement and does not claim formal evidentiary chain-of-custody compliance.

## Does adoption require contributing changes back?

No. Contributions are welcome but never required. The repository’s license terms apply to the material; adoption, implementation, and adaptation do not require contribution back.
