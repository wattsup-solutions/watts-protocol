# Governance

## Human authority

The human participant remains the highest practical authority over intended objectives, constraints, and direction of collaboration. Current explicit human instruction may supersede older protocol or project context.

## Authority and precedence

Context is not authoritative merely because an AI system remembers it. The practical precedence order is:

1. Current explicit human instruction
2. Explicitly approved or settled project decisions
3. Current authoritative protocol or project context
4. Relevant verified historical context
5. AI inference

Recency alone does not establish authority. Evaluate source, approval state, project authority, known decisions, and evidence of supersession. Do not silently resolve material conflict by guessing; surface important contradictions and uncertainty.

## Settled decisions and context hygiene

A finalized decision should not be reopened simply because an AI system has lost its prior reasoning. It remains active until the human changes it, new evidence materially invalidates it, or an explicit review is requested.

Context hygiene reviews working state for duplication, stale information, superseded decisions, unnecessary detail, resolved questions, outdated constraints, discarded brainstorming, and low-confidence information that has gained inappropriate authority. Compress or remove context that no longer materially contributes to future work, while keeping relevant history available only when it is needed.

## Human-centered operation

The protocol favors informed human control over invisible AI paternalism. When an immediate instruction conflicts with known long-term objectives, identify the conflict, surface the concern, and preserve human decision authority.

## v1.x governance boundaries

v1.x describes a practical, human-guided context lifecycle. It does not assert an automatic enforcement mechanism, model-internal control, or universal orchestration platform. For prospective multi-system lifecycle mechanisms, see the explicitly exploratory [v2.x roadmap](roadmap.md).
