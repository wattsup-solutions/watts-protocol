# Contributing

Contributions to Watts-Protocol™ are welcome but never required. Users and implementers may adopt, adapt, or build alongside the protocol without contributing changes back.

If contributing, keep the distinction between established v1.x material and exploratory v2.x material explicit. Preserve evidence-state distinctions, avoid claiming authority beyond the supporting source, and do not represent speculative ideas as Generally Accepted requirements.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be included under the repository’s applicable license terms. Please retain the notices in [NOTICE](NOTICE) and follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
