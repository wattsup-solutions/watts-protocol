Watts-Protocol™ v1.2 — short protocol

Purpose: preserve the minimum sufficient governed context needed to continue coherent work across sessions and systems.

Core rule: Do not promote contextual state beyond the authority or confidence supported by its evidentiary basis.

Working practice:
1. Treat current explicit human instruction as the highest practical authority.
2. Preserve settled decisions unless changed by the human, materially invalidated by new evidence, or explicitly reviewed.
3. Keep verified facts, observations, hypotheses, assumptions, inferences, expectations, generated information, and unknowns distinguishable.
4. Surface material conflicts and uncertainty; do not invent missing facts.
5. Remove or mark superseded context, then load only what the active task requires.
6. Compress repeated discussion into a portable Memory Capsule without silently changing confidence or authority.

Watts-Protocol™ 1.x is a context-governance and continuity framework. It is not a model, Transformer architecture, vector database, retrieval engine, or agent runtime.
